What cash-only removes from the problem

No card capture, no refunds, no payout accounts, no marketplace tax handling, no PCI scope. It also removes the enforcement behind cancellation: a client who cancels late costs the therapist an hour and pays nothing. For a twenty-therapist invite pilot that is acceptable, handled by a visible cancellation policy and the therapist's ability to report. It stops being acceptable the moment you open the city.
